Controls

Move: drag the left side, or A/D

Jump: the on-screen button, or Space

Attack: light / heavy buttons, or F / G

Mutations: bind up to four in the pack (Y), trigger with Q/E/R/T

Weapons: tap the top-right icons, or Z/X/C/V/B/N/M/,

Inventory: tap a slot, or number keys 1-9, or Y for the full pack

Forge: open the pack (Y) and tap "Open the Forge" to buy armor and enchant weapons

Map: tap the MAP button (teleport there once you've built Teleporting Advice)

Mine / Place: the pickaxe/block buttons, or left-click / right-click a tile

Teleporters: buy a block for 10 shards, place two, then WARP (K)

Dimensions: buy the Ember or Prism Portal for 100 shards each in the Build menu, then stand at the portal and tap PORTAL (O)

Online co-op: tap ONLINE, host an invite code or join a friend's world

Worlds: the WORLDS button saves and returns to your world list
